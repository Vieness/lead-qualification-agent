title: Loyalty mobile app for a coffee chain
services: [mobile, design]
industry: food and beverage
summary: iOS and Android loyalty app with QR rewards and push campaigns for a 40-store coffee chain; 120k users in the first year.
---

The client ran a paper stamp-card program that could not be measured. We designed and built a React Native app with QR-based points, personalised offers and push notifications tied to store visits. Integration with the POS system closed the loop between purchases and rewards.

Results: 120,000 registered users in 12 months, 18% higher visit frequency among members, and a campaign tool the marketing team runs without developers. Timeline: 14 weeks from discovery to store launch. Budget band: $40k–$60k.
