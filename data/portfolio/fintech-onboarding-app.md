services: [mobile, design]
industry: fintech
summary: Rebuilt the sign-up and identity verification flow of a mobile banking app, cutting onboarding time from 12 to 4 minutes.
---

Drop-off during sign-up was above 60%. We mapped the funnel, redesigned the onboarding screens and integrated a third-party KYC provider with document scanning and liveness checks in the existing Flutter app.

Results: onboarding completion rose from 38% to 71% and average time fell to 4 minutes. Timeline: 9 weeks. Budget band: $35k–$50k.
