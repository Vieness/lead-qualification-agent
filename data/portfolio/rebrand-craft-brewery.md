The brewery was moving from taprooms to retail and needed packaging that stands out on a shelf. We ran a brand workshop, created a new logo system, colour palette and illustration style, and designed a modular can label template for seasonal releases.

Results: listings in two national supermarket chains and 3x retail sales within six months. Timeline: 8 weeks. Budget band: $15k–$25k.
