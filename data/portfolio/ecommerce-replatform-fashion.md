services: [web, design]
industry: fashion retail
summary: Migrated a fashion store with 8,000 products to a headless e-commerce stack; page speed doubled and conversion rose 22%.
---

The legacy online shop was slow on mobile and hard to update. We moved catalogue, customers and order history to a headless e-commerce platform with a Next.js storefront, rebuilt product listing and checkout flows, and set up redirects to keep SEO traffic.

Results: Largest Contentful Paint dropped from 4.8s to 1.9s, mobile conversion rose 22%, and the content team now publishes collections without developer help. Timeline: 16 weeks. Budget band: $50k–$80k.
