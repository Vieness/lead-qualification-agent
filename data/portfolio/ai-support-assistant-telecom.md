The support team handled 30,000 chats a month, most of them repetitive. We built a retrieval-augmented assistant over the operator's knowledge base with guardrails against off-topic and unsafe answers, and a hand-off to human agents with the full conversation context.

Results: 46% of chats resolved without a human, average first response under 5 seconds, and customer satisfaction unchanged. Timeline: 12 weeks. Budget band: $60k–$90k.
