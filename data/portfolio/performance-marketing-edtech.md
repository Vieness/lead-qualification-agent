The school relied on one paid channel with rising costs. We rebuilt tracking and attribution, launched search and paid social campaigns with creative testing, and set up landing page A/B tests for each course.

Results: cost per enrolment down 41%, enrolments up 2.3x over a quarter, and a weekly reporting dashboard for the founders. Ongoing engagement, $8k–$12k per month plus ad spend.
