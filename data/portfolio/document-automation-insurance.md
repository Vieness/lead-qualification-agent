Claims handlers spent hours re-typing data from PDFs and photos. We built an extraction pipeline with an LLM and deterministic validation rules, a review queue for low-confidence fields, and an integration with the broker's claims system.

Results: manual data entry cut by 70%, processing time per claim down from 2 days to 4 hours, with every automated decision logged for audit. Timeline: 10 weeks. Budget band: $45k–$70k.
