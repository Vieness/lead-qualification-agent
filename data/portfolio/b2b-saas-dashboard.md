A logistics SaaS start-up needed a customer-facing dashboard before its Series A. We ran user interviews with dispatchers, designed the information architecture and built a React web app with live charts, alerts on SLA breaches and CSV exports.

Results: shipped an MVP in 10 weeks, demo used in the funding round, and churn in the pilot group fell by a third. Budget band: $30k–$45k.
