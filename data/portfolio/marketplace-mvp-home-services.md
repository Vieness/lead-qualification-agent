A start-up wanted to validate a marketplace for home repairs in one city. We built a web app for customers and a mobile app for professionals with booking, in-app payments, ratings and an admin panel for vetting.

Results: launched in 12 weeks, 400 professionals onboarded and 2,500 jobs booked in the first three months. Budget band: $70k–$100k.
